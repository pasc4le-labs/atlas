# PAST plans

Rolling archive of finished plans. Newest first. **Never exceed ~2,000
tokens** (~8 KB / ~1,000 words): when a new entry would push the file over
the cap, drop the oldest (bottom) entries to fit. Prepend a short entry
per finished plan (number, title, one-line summary, date), then remove the
plan's directory.
